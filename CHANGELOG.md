Welcome to the foundational release of the documentation hub (`v1.0.0`). This update establishes our core architecture, localization infrastructure, and unifies our content strategy.

- [49ae855](https://github.com/tupynambalucas-dev/docs/commit/49ae855) feat(content): unify content architecture, merging changelog and blog into a single chronological timeline ([@tupynambalucas](https://github.com/tupynambalucas))
